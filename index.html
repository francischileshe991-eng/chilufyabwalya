10 Cyber Security Tips for UNZA Students

  1. Use only official UNZA student WIFI (Eduroam) & limit use of public Wi-Fi.
  2. Use strong passwords on UNZA-SIS, UNZA-Email, Moodle & don’t share with others
  3. Do not respond to suspicious emails (fake UNZA notices)
  4. Keep apps & software updated
  5. Protect your NRC & student ID info
  6. Be careful when shopping online & protect your PIN at all times
  7. Stay alert to scams & fraud on WhatsApp groups offering rooms and other services
  8. Be a responsible digital citizen – do not scam others
  9. Enable two-factor authentication
  10. Avoid oversharing PII on social media
